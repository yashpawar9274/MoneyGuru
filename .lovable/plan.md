# Home credit-card balance and quick adjustments

## What will change
- Add a credit-card summary directly below the home balance, showing each card's remaining limit and total available credit.
- Include available card credit in the home total, while separately labeling cash balance and card availability so the number is understandable.
- Add `+` and `−` controls on every card: `+` records a card payment and increases remaining credit; `−` records a card expense and reduces remaining credit.
- Keep the full credit-card screen accessible from the home summary for adding cards and viewing details.

## Interaction
- Tapping either control opens a small amount form for the selected card.
- `+` saves a repayment; `−` saves an expense linked to that card.
- Validate positive amounts, show success/error feedback, and refresh totals immediately.

## Verification
- Check the home screen at mobile and desktop widths.
- Verify card expense decreases available credit and card payment increases it.
- Run the relevant checks and update the roadmap item.
